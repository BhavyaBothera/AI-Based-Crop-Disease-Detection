Evaluation Protocol & Scope Note Scientific Integrity & Dataset Context

Evaluation Methodology: Metrics reflect benchmark evaluation on strictly held-out laboratory photographs with uniform backgrounds from the PlantVillage dataset (70% train / 15% validation / 15% test partition; 3,413 test images completely isolated from all model training and validation phases, with validation Macro-F1 at 0.9686 across 3,418 images). While these figures demonstrate high neural convergence under controlled backgrounds, performance in real-world agricultural environments may vary due to complex canopy backgrounds, variable natural lighting, leaf occlusions, or multi-pathogen co-infections. The Level B suitability gate is engineered to reject ambiguous inputs rather than force an ungrounded classification.

Core System Architecture

Engineered Diagnostic Capabilities

A comprehensive clinical AgriTech tool engineered with defensible machine learning principles, fail-closed safety, and transparent agronomic guidance.

Two-Tier Input Validation

Pre-inference verification screens image resolution, byte health, and aspect ratios (Level A), before a learned foliar gate (Level B) filters out non-leaf photos, hands, tools, and non-target vegetation.

🛡️ Level A + B Safeguards

Class-Weighted Neural Inference

Optimized MobileNetV2 architecture trained with inverse-frequency class weighting to protect diagnostic sensitivity on minority disease classes without sacrificing overall precision.

🧠 MobileNetV2 Backbone

Calibrated Confidence Tiers

Raw softmax scores are categorized into High (≥90%), Moderate (70–90%), and Low (<70%) confidence bands, with clear contextual warnings when uncertainty warrants field inspection.

⚡ Three-Tier Confidence

Differential Candidate Classes

Surfaces the Top-3 candidate predictions with relative probability distribution bars, enabling agronomists to evaluate visually similar symptom complexes like Early Blight versus Septoria.
