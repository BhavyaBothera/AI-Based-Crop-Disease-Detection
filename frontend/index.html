Diagnostic Architecture

How PHYTOSIGNAL Evaluates Foliage

Every submission passes through a rigorous multi-stage pipeline designed to prevent false diagnoses and protect against out-of-distribution hallucinations.

STEP 01

Physical Integrity Gate

Uploaded files are read in bounded 64KB chunks up to a 10MB limit. Binary magic bytes are sniffed to verify genuine JPEG, PNG, or WebP formats, and decompression bomb protection caps pixel decoding at 20 Megapixels.

LAYER A // RESOURCE DEFENSE
STEP 02

Leaf Acceptance Gate

A dedicated 3-class classifier (supported leaf, other plant, non-leaf) screens the image. Arbitrary human portraits, animals, and objects are rejected with 99.8%+ confidence, while unsupported plant species are politely declined.

LAYER B // LEAF_GATE.KERAS
STEP 03

Neural Classification & IPM

Verified foliar specimens enter a balanced class-weighted MobileNetV2 network trained on 15 Solanaceae conditions. The prediction links to our verified Integrated Pest Management knowledge base.
