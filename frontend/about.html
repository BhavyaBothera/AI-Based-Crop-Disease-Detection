PHYTOSIGNAL is an independent AgriTech platform engineered to solve the most pervasive failure in applied computer vision: out-of-distribution hallucination under closed-world assumptions.

1. The Closed-World Softmax Vulnerability

Standard deep learning image classifiers employ a final softmax normalization layer where the predicted class probabilities strictly sum to 1.0. When an arbitrary image containing zero plant tissue—such as a human portrait, a landscape, a domestic pet, or a document—is fed into such a model, the network cannot abstain. The argmax operation forcibly assigns the input to whichever class matches random latent noise, frequently with deceptively high confidence (e.g. 99%+ on a human selfie).

In agricultural decision support, a false diagnosis on non-leaf imagery is dangerous. Presenting chemical fungicide recommendations for a non-leaf subject erodes trust and undermines agronomic integrity.

2. Two-Tier Input Safety & Acceptance Pipeline

To eradicate OOD vulnerability, PHYTOSIGNAL establishes two sequential, decoupled validation gates before any tensor is permitted to reach the disease classification head:

LAYER A

Physical & DoS Integrity

Enforces a streamed chunked 10 MB payload ceiling. Validates binary magic bytes (JPEG/PNG/WEBP). Limits decoded dimensions to 20 Megapixels and enforces a 64×64 pixel floor. Analyzes luminance standard deviation (σ ≤ 2.0) to eliminate uniform canvases.

LAYER B

Leaf Acceptance Gate

A 3-class linear classifier (supported_leaf, other_plant, non_leaf) trained on frozen ImageNet MobileNetV2 features across 25,000+ real photographs. Demands a minimum 0.60 confidence floor.

LAYER C

Deterministic Fallback

If neural gate weights are offline, a vectorized HSV plant-pigmentation mask (Hue 40°–170°, Sat ≥ 0.12, Val ≥ 0.08) and connected-component area checks guarantee that non-vegetation images are never silently passed to inference.

When an upload fails screening, the server terminates processing immediately with an HTTP 422 Unprocessable Content structured payload (InputRejectedError). Diagnostic fields, top candidates, and chemical advisories are completely withheld.

4. Responsible AI & Agronomic Reality

A high test-set score on controlled agricultural datasets does not translate directly into 100% diagnostic certainty in the field. Real-world foliar symptoms are influenced by multiple compounding factors:

  • Co-Infection & Mixed Etiology: A leaf may simultaneously suffer from fungal lesions (e.g. Early Blight) and nutrient deficiencies (e.g. Nitrogen chlorosis).
  • Environmental Stressors: Drought, hail damage, pesticide phytotoxicity, and wind scorching can visually mimic pathogenic lesions.
  • Lighting & Capture Angle: Specular reflections, direct sunlight glare, and shadows alter color distributions.

For these reasons, PHYTOSIGNAL is architected as an agronomic decision-support instrument, not an autonomous applicator. Users are explicitly directed to confirm foliar diagnoses with regional university agricultural extension personnel or licensed crop advisors before purchasing or applying commercial chemical controls.
