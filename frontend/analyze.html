Foliar Capture Best Practices

  • Supported Crops: Provide foliage from Tomato, Potato, or Bell Pepper plants.
  • Framing: Center a single plant leaf occupying at least 60% of the image frame.
  • Lighting: Ensure even, diffuse illumination; avoid harsh glare, deep shadows, or extreme blur.
  • Background: Position against a plain or natural soil background free of faces, pets, or non-botanical objects.
